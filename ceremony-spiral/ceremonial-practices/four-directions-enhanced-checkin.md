# Four Directions Enhanced Ceremonial Check-In

**Purpose:** Regular structured reflection grounded in Four Directions and Medicine Wheel teachings.

**Principle:** Ceremony isn't just what we build; it's how we build. The Four Directions guide our practice.

**Integration:** This enhanced template deepens the original ceremonial check-in with Indigenous knowledge systems.

---

## Weekly Team Check-In (35-40 minutes, every Friday)

### Opening: Sacred Space Creation (3 minutes)

**Facilitator begins:**

> "We create sacred space for this gathering. Though we may be in different physical locations, we come together in ceremony."

**Land Acknowledgment (each person):**

> "I acknowledge that I gather on the traditional territories of [Indigenous peoples]. I honor the ancestors of this land and commit to being a good guest/relative."

**Facilitator reads:**

> "We acknowledge the Four Directions that guide our work:
> **East** - where the sun rises, bringing vision and new beginnings
> **South** - where we grow, build relationships, and plan together
> **West** - where we reflect, learn from experience, and honor mystery
> **North** - where wisdom resides, completion happens, and we integrate what we've learned"

**Each person shares:**
- One word: How are you arriving to this space?

---

## Part 1: East Direction - Vision & Intention (5 minutes)

**The East asks:** "What vision guided our work this week?"

### Reflection Questions:

1. **Did we remember our intention?**
   - What was the vision we set for this week?
   - Did we stay true to it, or did we get pulled away?

2. **What new beginnings happened?**
   - What did we start this week?
   - How did we honor the dawn energy of new things?

3. **Did we see clearly?**
   - Were we visionary or reactive?
   - Did we look ahead or only at what's immediately in front of us?

**Team shares:** Brief reflections on vision and clarity this week

---

## Part 2: South Direction - Relationships & Growth (10 minutes)

**The South asks:** "How did we honor relationships and grow together?"

### Relational Accountability Check:

> "This week, did our actions honor the relationships we committed to?"

**Each team member reflects:**
- ✅ **What honored relationships this week?**
  - With each other (team)?
  - With community partners?
  - With pilot customers?
  - With the technology itself (did we build relationally)?

- ⚠️ **Where did we fall short?**
  - What relationships did we neglect?
  - Where did we prioritize efficiency over relationship?
  - What harm did we cause (even unintentionally)?

- 🔄 **What needs tending?**
  - Which relationships need repair?
  - Which relationships need deepening?
  - What will we do differently next week?

### Growth Assessment:

> "Did we grow this week, or just work?"

- What capacity did we develop?
- What did we learn together (not just individually)?
- How did our relationships deepen?

**Examples:**
- ✅ "We delayed a technical decision to get community input first" (honoring relationship)
- ⚠️ "We made a GitHub integration choice without considering accessibility" (relationship with users neglected)
- 🔄 "Next week, we'll include Ava in technical architecture discussions early" (tending relationship)

---

## Part 3: West Direction - Reflection & Learning (10 minutes)

**The West asks:** "What did we learn from experience?"

### Sacred Pause Check:

> "Did we create space for reflection, or did we rush?"

**Team reflects:**

1. **Did we pause before major decisions?**
   - Where did we honor the sacred pause?
   - Where did we rush and regret it?

2. **What did experience teach us?**
   - What worked unexpectedly well?
   - What failed in ways we can learn from?
   - What did the week reveal about our assumptions?

3. **Did we honor the mystery?**
   - Where did things unfold in ways we couldn't control?
   - Did we try to force outcomes, or did we work with what emerged?

4. **Wound Witnessing:**
   - Where did we cause harm this week (to team, community, ourselves)?
   - Can we acknowledge it without defensiveness?
   - What does this wound teach us?

**Examples:**
- ✅ "Before committing to the MCP server architecture, we paused to consider alternatives" (sacred pause honored)
- ⚠️ "We rushed the pilot customer outreach; didn't think through the ask" (rushed past pause)
- 🔄 "Build in 'thinking time' before Friday meetings" (adjust for next week)

### Perspective Balance:

> "Did we honor multiple ways of knowing in our work this week?"

**Team reflects:**
- Which perspectives were well-represented in our decisions?
  - Technical? Community? Business? Partnership? Research? Product?
- Which perspectives were missing?
- How do we bring in those missing voices next week?

---

## Part 4: North Direction - Wisdom & Integration (8 minutes)

**The North asks:** "What wisdom have we gained? How do we integrate it?"

### Decisions Review:

**Pull from decision log:**
```bash
grep '"timestamp":"2025-11-' decision-log.jsonl | tail -7
```

**For each decision:**
- Does the team agree this is accurate?
- What wisdom informed this decision?
- Any decisions we forgot to document?

### Eight Feelings Wheel Assessment:

**Quick team health check through developmental lens:**

> "As a team, how are we doing across the eight feelings?"

| Feeling | Age Stage | Team Assessment | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|-------|
| **Trust** | 1-3 | ✅ ⚠️ 🚨 | Do we trust each other and the process? |
| **Autonomy** | 3-6 | ✅ ⚠️ 🚨 | Does each person have appropriate agency? |
| **Initiative** | 5-8 | ✅ ⚠️ 🚨 | Are we proactively contributing? |
| **Accomplishment** | 8-18 | ✅ ⚠️ 🚨 | Are we celebrating achievements properly? |
| **Identity** | 18-25 | ✅ ⚠️ 🚨 | Does each person know their role and value? |
| **Intimacy** | 25-35 | ✅ ⚠️ 🚨 | Are relationships deepening or staying surface? |
| **Generativity** | 35-55 | ✅ ⚠️ 🚨 | Are we creating something meaningful beyond ourselves? |
| **Integrity** | 55+ | ✅ ⚠️ 🚨 | Are we practicing what we preach? |

**Red flags:** Any 🚨 needs immediate attention

### Commitments for Next Week:

**Team agrees:**
- What are we committing to?
- Who is responsible?
- What support do they need?
- How does this serve the larger vision?

---

## Part 5: Celebration & Gratitude (3 minutes)

**Medicine Wheel Closing:**

> "The Medicine Wheel teaches us that all directions work together in balance."

**Each person shares:**

1. **One celebration:** What went well this week that we should honor?
   - (Honors South: growth and accomplishment)

2. **One learning:** What did we learn that will serve us going forward?
   - (Honors West: wisdom from experience)

**Examples:**
- Celebration: "Jerry's pilot customer outreach was excellent; clear and respectful"
- Learning: "We learned that Anthropic's partnership team prefers brief concept papers over long proposals"

---

## Closing: Integration & Return to the Circle (2 minutes)

**Facilitator reads:**

> "We've traveled the Four Directions together:
> - **East:** We remembered our vision
> - **South:** We tended our relationships
> - **West:** We learned from experience
> - **North:** We integrated wisdom
>
> We return to the center, where all directions meet. We carry what we've learned. We commit to continuing in ceremony."

**Each person:** One word for next week's intention.

**Facilitator closes:**

> "We release this sacred space with gratitude. We return to our work, guided by the Four Directions. Aho/All my relations."

## Monthly Synthesis (90 minutes, last Friday of month)

### Opening: Extended Sacred Space Creation (10 minutes)

**Land Acknowledgment (as above)**

**Facilitator offers option:**

> "We'll begin with a brief meditation to ground us in this monthly synthesis. If meditation is not part of your practice, you're welcome to hold your own silent reflection."

**Elder Meditation (5 minutes):**

> "Close your eyes if comfortable. Take three deep breaths.
> Imagine the ancestors of this work—both human ancestors and the ancestors of these ideas.
> What wisdom do they offer about the month we've just completed?
> What do they want us to remember about the work ahead?
> Listen for their guidance..."
>
> [5 minutes of silence]
>
> "Gently return your awareness to this space. Thank the ancestors for their presence."

---

### East Direction Review: Vision Alignment (15 minutes)

**The month's intention:**

> "At the beginning of this month, what vision were we holding?"

**Review:**
- Did we stay aligned with that vision?
- Where did we drift?
- Where did the vision evolve (and should we honor that evolution)?

**Decisions alignment check:**
```bash
grep '"timestamp":"2025-11-' decision-log.jsonl > november-decisions.jsonl
cat november-decisions.jsonl | jq -r '.decision' | head -20
```

**Team discusses:**
- Do these decisions reflect our vision?
- Or do they show we got pulled into reactive mode?

**New vision for next month:**
- What vision do we set for the month ahead?
- How will we remember it when we get busy?

---

### South Direction Review: Relational Accountability Audit (30 minutes)

**Major relationship review:**

#### Commitments Made:

Pull all decisions with `relational_impact` from this month:

**For each commitment:**
- Did we honor what we said we would?
- Who were we accountable to?
- Did we maintain that accountability?

#### Create Relational Accountability Report:

```markdown
## [Month Year] Relational Accountability Report

### Commitments Kept:
- [Decision ID]: We said we would [X], and we did [Y]
- How this honored relationship: [...]

### Commitments Adjusted:
- [Decision ID]: We planned [X], but learned [Y], so we adjusted to [Z]
- Rationale: [Why adjustment honored relationships better]
- Who we consulted: [...]

### Commitments We're Struggling With:
- [Decision ID]: We said [X], but we haven't yet because [Y]
- Plan: [How we'll address this next month]
- Who we need to involve: [...]

### Relationships Deepened This Month:
- [Which relationships grew stronger and why]

### Relationships Needing Repair:
- [Where we caused harm, even unintentionally]
- [How we'll make amends]
```

#### Community Voice Assessment:

> "Did Indigenous and community voices shape our work this month, or were they peripheral?"

**Review:**
- How many decisions included "community" perspective? (Count from decision log)
- How many times did we reach out to community partners?
- How many times did community feedback change our course?
- **Honest question:** Is community input shaping the product, or just validating our ideas?

**Assessment:**
- ✅ Community voice was central to [X decisions/features]
- ⚠️ Community voice was peripheral to [Y decisions/features]
- 🔄 Next month, we will [specific changes]

### West Direction Review: Learning & Reflection (20 minutes)

**What did experience teach us this month?**

#### Perspective Balance Analysis:

Generate perspective distribution from decision log:

```bash
cat november-decisions.jsonl | jq '.perspectives_considered | keys[]' | sort | uniq -c
```

**Analyze:**

| Perspective | Count | Percentage | Assessment |
|-------------|-------|------------|------------|
| Technical | [#] | [%] | ✅ ⚠️ 🚨 |
| Community | [#] | [%] | ✅ ⚠️ 🚨 |
| Business | [#] | [%] | ✅ ⚠️ 🚨 |
| Partnership | [#] | [%] | ✅ ⚠️ 🚨 |
| Research | [#] | [%] | ✅ ⚠️ 🚨 |
| Product | [#] | [%] | ✅ ⚠️ 🚨 |

**Reflections:**

1. **Over-represented perspectives:** Why are we favoring this? Appropriate for this phase, or avoiding other voices?

2. **Under-represented perspectives:** Why is this voice missing? What do we need to do to include it?

3. **Balance adjustments:** What specific changes for next month?

#### Wound Witnessing (Monthly):

**Facilitator asks:**

> "This month, where did we cause harm? Let's acknowledge it without defensiveness."

**Team reflects:**
- Harm to team members (overwork, exclusion, dismissal)?
- Harm to community partners (extraction, tokenization, delay)?
- Harm to pilot customers (unmet commitments, poor communication)?
- Harm to ourselves (burnout, integrity compromises)?

**For each harm:**
- Acknowledge it clearly
- Understand root cause (structural, not just individual)
- Plan repair (not just apology)
- Adjust structures to prevent recurrence

### North Direction Review: Wisdom & Integration (20 minutes)

**What wisdom have we gained?**

#### Decisions Revisited:

**Pull decisions due for revisit this month:**

```bash
cat november-decisions.jsonl | jq 'select(.reversibility.revisit_date <= "2025-11-30")'
```

**For each:**
- Do we keep this decision?
- Do we adjust it based on what we've learned?
- Do we reverse it because circumstances changed?
- **Document the revisit** (create new decision entry or update status)

#### Structural Tension Assessment:

**Robert Fritz framework:**

```markdown
## Structural Tension: [Month Year]

### Desired Outcome (what we said we wanted at month start):
- Charter framework agreed
- Community partnerships started
- Technical architecture drafted
- Pilot customer interested

### Current Reality (where we actually are):
- Charter: [Status - be honest]
- Community: [Status - be honest]
- Technical: [Status - be honest]
- Pilot: [Status - be honest]

### Gap Analysis:
- What's working to close the gap? [Reinforcing structures]
- What's preventing us from closing the gap? [Limiting structures]
- Are we operating from desired outcome, or reacting to current reality?
```

**Team discusses:**
- Are we trying to force progress?
- Or are we creating structures that naturally generate progress?

### Medicine Wheel Integration: All Four Directions in Balance (10 minutes)

**Facilitator asks:**

> "The Medicine Wheel teaches that all four directions must be in balance. Let's assess our balance this month."

**Team reflects:**

| Direction | Strong This Month? | Weak This Month? | How to Balance? |
|-----------|-------------------|------------------|-----------------|
| **East (Vision)** | ✅ ⚠️ 🚨 | [Notes] | [Actions] |
| **South (Relationships)** | ✅ ⚠️ 🚨 | [Notes] | [Actions] |
| **West (Reflection)** | ✅ ⚠️ 🚨 | [Notes] | [Actions] |
| **North (Wisdom)** | ✅ ⚠️ 🚨 | [Notes] | [Actions] |

**Common imbalances:**
- Too much **East** (vision) without **West** (reflection) = Rushing forward without learning
- Too much **South** (doing/building) without **North** (integration) = Activity without wisdom
- Too much **West** (reflection) without **South** (action) = Analysis paralysis
- Too much **North** (wisdom) without **East** (vision) = Stuck in what we know

**Adjustments for next month:**
- If East strong/West weak: Schedule more reflection time
- If South strong/North weak: Schedule integration sessions
- If West strong/South weak: Push ourselves to act despite uncertainty
- If North strong/East weak: Create space for new vision

**Each person shares:**

> "We've walked the Four Directions together this month:
> - We set vision in the **East**
> - We tended relationships in the **South**
> - We learned from experience in the **West**
> - We integrated wisdom in the **North**
>
> The Medicine Wheel turns. The cycle continues. We carry what we've learned into the next moon.
>
> All my relations. Aho."
