## Technical Scaffolding for Distributed Teams

**Shared Infrastructure Needed:**

1. **Decision Log (JSONL)**
   - Every decision: who, what, why, perspectives honored, relational impact
   - Queryable by: decision type, team member, date, relational impact
   - Monthly synthesis: "How are we maintaining accountability?"

2. **Ceremonial Check-Ins (Markdown)**
   - Weekly: "Did we practice what we preach this week?"
   - Team reflection: What was honoring? What needs adjustment?
   - Action: Specific changes to maintain relational integrity

3. **Community Feedback Integration**
   - Real-time channel for Lian to surface community input
   - Technical team's response: "How do we integrate this?"
   - Documented decision: "We changed X because of community feedback"

4. **Relational Accountability Dashboard**
   - Metrics: agency increase, community satisfaction, ethical alignment
   - Visible to: entire team, pilot customers, Indigenous communities
   - Review: Weekly by leadership, monthly by full team

## Why This Model Works for Ceremony Spiral

**Distributed Expertise Honors Relationality:**
- Not top-down command (traditional org chart)
- Interdependent nodes (everyone's work affects everyone)
- Regular synchronization (relational check-ins)
- Multiple perspectives preserved (never forced to one view)
- Accountability flows both directions

**It Matches the Vision:**
- We're building systems with circular relational dynamics
- So we're building the team using circular relational dynamics
- Practice what we preach, at every level

**It's Scalable:**
- Add more nodes (more communities, more expertise areas)
- Same protocol structures work
- Relational accountability scales with addition of people

**It Positions Everyone:**
- William: Strategic architect of multi-system portfolio
- Jerry: Delivery leader & team builder
- Each team member: Deep expertise area + contribution to something meaningful

This is how mid-career mastery + emerging talent + Indigenous principles + enterprise technology = market advantage.
